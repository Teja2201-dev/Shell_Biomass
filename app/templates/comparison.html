Architectural Insights: Why Does the Hybrid Ensemble Outperform?

1. Bagging + Boosting Synergy

Random Forest excels at broad pattern bagging, while XGBoost targets residual gradient errors. Blending both prevents overfitting while capturing localized agro-climatic relationships.

2. Extreme Randomization (ExtraTrees)

ExtraTrees (weighted at 0.60) picks random cut-point thresholds instead of greedy optimal splits, providing significant variance reduction against climatic outliers and sensor noise.

3. 12.6% Reduction in Absolute Error

MAE dropped from 81.36 kg/ha (XGBoost) and 76.11 kg/ha (Random Forest) down to 71.08 kg/ha in the Hybrid Model, offering reliable biomass forecasts for bioenergy plant feedstock planning.
