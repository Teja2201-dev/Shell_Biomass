Scientific Agro-Climatic Takeaways

Temperature Sensitivity

Mean Temperature is the single most dominant factor (|SHAP| = 76.6 kg/ha). Elevated thermal stress above 28°C during the flowering and pod-filling phase sharply curtails pod production and resulting shell biomass.

Relative Humidity & Moisture

Relative Humidity and root-zone soil wetness are critical secondary drivers. Moderate humidity (60-70%) coupled with adequate root moisture supports optimal pod development and healthy shell lignification.

Satellite Canopy Greenness

MODIS NDVI and EVI provide real-time canopy density signals. Peak vegetative greenness is positively correlated with total biomass yield, enabling accurate remote estimation without manual ground harvesting surveys.
